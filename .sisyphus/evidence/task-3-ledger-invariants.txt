--- LEDGER INVARIANTS (TARGET SPECIFICATION) ---
The following invariants must hold for all cohorts and users:

1. INVARIANT: 0 <= claimed <= accrued <= entitlement
   - claimed: Amount already withdrawn by user
   - accrued: Carry amount allocated to user
   - entitlement: Gross entitlement before carry deduction

2. INVARIANT: carryRemaining = accrued - claimed
   - Tracks remaining claimable amount
   - Must never go negative

3. COHORT CONSERVATION:
   cohortTotalClaimed + cohortCarryRemaining == cohortTotalEntitlement
   (within rounding tolerance of 10 wei)

4. COHORT ACCRUAL:
   cohortTotalAccrued == cohortTotalClaimed + cohortCarryRemaining
   (within rounding tolerance of 10 wei)
