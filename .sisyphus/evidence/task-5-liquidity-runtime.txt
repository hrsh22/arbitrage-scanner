2. LIABILITY-FIRST BEHAVIOR ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Pattern: Recall Before Deploy
- Pre-settlement: rebalanceCapital called with pendingWithdrawalLiability
  → Provider recalls capital from Safe to Vault to cover withdrawals
  → Ensures sufficient funds for redemption claims
  
- Post-settlement: rebalanceCapital called with 0n liability
  → Queue already processed, liabilities settled
  → Deploys available capital back to Safe for trading

Implication: No Gradual Realization
- All liability resolution happens at settlement boundary
- No partial recall/deploy cycles between epochs
- Supports boundary-only position model
