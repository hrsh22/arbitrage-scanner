Initial State (after settlement):
- cohortTotalAccrued = SUM(request.accrued) = Total entitlement
- cohortTotalClaimed = 0
- cohortCarryRemaining = cohortTotalAccrued

After Partial Claim (amount X):
- cohortTotalClaimed = X
- cohortCarryRemaining = cohortTotalAccrued - X
- cohortTotalAccrued unchanged
- Check: X + (cohortTotalAccrued - X) = cohortTotalAccrued ✓

After Loss (amount L):
- cohortTotalAccrued -= L
- cohortCarryRemaining -= L
- cohortTotalClaimed unchanged
- Check: cohortTotalClaimed + cohortCarryRemaining = cohortTotalAccrued ✓

After Full Claim:
- cohortTotalClaimed = cohortTotalAccrued
- cohortCarryRemaining = 0
- Check: cohortTotalAccrued + 0 = cohortTotalAccrued ✓
