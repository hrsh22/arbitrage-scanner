T3 Evidence: Escrowed Shares Behavior
======================================

Verification Date: 2026-03-11
Contract: contracts/src/ClosedBookBatchVault.sol

ESCROW MECHANISM:
-----------------
Function: requestRedeem(uint256 shares, address controller, address owner)

1. Shares are transferred from owner to vault (escrow)
   IERC20(address(this)).safeTransferFrom(owner, address(this), shares);

2. Shares remain in vault balance until settlement
   - NOT burned immediately
   - Total supply unchanged
   - Shares are "economically live" until settlement

3. RedemptionRequest status starts as "Pending"
   - Transitions to "Escrowed" during flattenBatch()
   - Transitions to "Claimable" during settlement
   - Final state: "Claimed" after redeem()

BURN TIMING:
------------
Shares are ONLY burned during redeem() or withdraw():
- _burn(address(this), shares);

This happens AFTER settlement when user claims their assets.
Until then, escrowed shares count toward total supply.

ECONOMIC PARTICIPATION:
-----------------------
Escrowed shares participate in cycle economics because:
1. They are still in circulation (totalSupply unchanged)
2. They receive the same batch clearing price as active shares
3. Settlement calculates claimable assets based on shares * clearingPrice
