4. SAFETY PROPERTIES
================================================================================

P1: Deterministic Settlement
    Settlement calculation is deterministic based on frozen snapshot values.
    No external parameter can alter the economic outcome after freeze.

P2: Mutation Resistance
    Runtime changes to totalAssets() or totalSharesPending do NOT affect
    settlement of frozen epochs. Each epoch is economically isolated.

P3: Audit Trail
    Both snapshot values are stored on-chain and publicly verifiable:
    - epochs[epochId].totalSharesPendingAtFreeze
    - epochs[epochId].totalAssetsAvailableAtFreeze
